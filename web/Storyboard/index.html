🏆 Welcome to Liga-Kampus

University Multi-Sport Management Platform

Liga-Kampus is a centralized university sports platform designed for students, team managers, organizers, and administrators. The system helps manage multiple sports competitions, live matches, player performances, standings, and tournament activities in one place.

⚽ Multi-Sport

Manage football, basketball, badminton, tennis and more.

📊 Analytics

Track goals, assists, cards, substitutions and player statistics.

👥 User Roles

Students, organizers, managers and admins with permissions.
